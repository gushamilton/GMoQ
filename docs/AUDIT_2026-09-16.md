## Tier 1 - the default changed and the rest of the pipeline did not

### 1. `prepare_matched_weights.R` cannot build weights for the new default. No guard catches the mismatch.

**The defect.** Stage 1's default is now `--loco-mode covariate` (`dfa0448`,
`053fc62`), which leaves `Y` unadjusted, so the Stage 2 weight matrix must be
built on **raw** `Y`. The only weight-preparation script in the tree,
`scripts/stage2/prepare_matched_weights.R`, makes `--loco` **mandatory**
(`stop_usage()` at :23-33, the `is.null` check at :57-59, the `file.exists`
check at :88-91) and hard-codes

```r
:332   y_adj  <- as.numeric(ph$y) - scale_factor * pred_target
:339   y_base <- as.numeric(y_adj)
```

as the baseline from which `q_tau`, `sd` and the whole family fit are taken.
There is no `--loco-mode`, no way to suppress the subtraction, and no other
script that builds `W`.

**It is silent.** `scripts/stage2/run_stage2_chr.R:117-129` validates weight
provenance for *phenotype* and *chromosome* only. Stage 1 records `loco_mode` in
the `Sigma_0` sidecar (`cli.py:2081-2087`) and the pooled path checks that
sidecar against the Stage 1 covariance metadata — Stage 1 against Stage 1. **No
code anywhere compares the weights' baseline to the scan's `loco_mode`.** A
subtract-mode `W` on a covariate-mode scan passes every check and produces a
number.

**The doc says to do the impossible.** `LOCO_AS_COVARIATE.md` "Reproducing":
*"the Stage 2 weights builder is invoked without `--loco`"* — which the script
refuses.

**Size of the error.** Not negligible: `var(y - 1.09*loco)/var(y) = 0.976`
(`LOCO_MIXTURE_HAZARD.md`), so the baseline `sd` is ~1.2% off before the mixture
is refitted; for the skew/mixture families `p1, mu1, mu2, sd1, sd2` all move, and
every column of `W` with them.

**Missing.** Either a `--loco-mode` switch on the script (a few lines), or a
provenance field recording the baseline mode plus a refusal in
`run_stage2_chr.R`. **Cost: under an hour.** This is the one item that can
silently produce wrong published numbers from the shipped default.

### 6. The permutation does not show what the docs say it shows, and its own number contradicts "calibrated".

`LOCO_MIXTURE_HAZARD.md` "Genomic inflation: measured":

```
permuted, true null:   lambda gamma 0.999 | beta_left 0.904 | beta_right 0.993
```
concluded as **"The estimator is calibrated and slightly conservative"**, and
then used to license *"the real-data lambda of 1.35-1.49 is genuine chr22 signal,
not miscalibration"*.

Two problems, one of them arithmetic:

**(a) `lambda = 0.904` is not "slightly".** Shuffling the phenotype across
421,405 people destroys the association but not the sampling distribution. At
17,550 variants the Monte Carlo SE on lambda is order 0.01, so 0.904 sits roughly
**nine standard errors** below 1. It implies `beta_left`'s standard errors are
about **5% too large** (`1/sqrt(0.904) = 1.052`), systematically, on the null.
That is *larger than several effects the project is currently arguing about*: the
1-3% quadrature gap in `EFFICIENCY_BOUND.md`, the 0-6% OLS projection penalty,
the ~3% gamma deconfounding signal in H12. `EFFICIENCY_BOUND.md`'s decomposition
of the female 12% closes with "unexplained x1.011" while the project's own
permutation says there is a 5% conservative bias sitting in that same parameter.
The asymmetry — 0.904 for `beta_left`, 0.993 and 0.999 for the other two — is
itself unexplained and is the shape of a `W`-column or density problem, not noise.

**(b) The permutation cannot speak to relatedness at all.** Shuffling `Y` across
people severs the phenotype from the relatedness structure along with the signal.
A relatedness-driven inflation vanishes *by construction*. The permutation
bounds one thing well — estimator/SE self-consistency — and says nothing about
structure control. "The real-data lambda is genuine chr22 signal, not
miscalibration" is a two-way inference from a one-way experiment; H1's masking
result is the evidence that actually supports it, and the permutation is not.

**Missing.** An explanation for 0.904, and a restatement that scopes the
permutation to estimator calibration. **Cost: the restatement is free; explaining
0.904 needs the permuted output re-examined per tau (small).**

## Tier 4 - attacks on today's four conclusions

### A. "pooled and jackknife are bit-identical under OLS" — TRUE, but it is not a measurement and it is not the question.

`gwas.R` computes `A <- solve(t(W) %*% W, t(W))` **before** the `se_mode` branch;
under `tau_cov_estimator == "ols"` the `tau_cov` branch touches only `SE_params`
(`:459-467`) and `param_cov_upper`. `params <- A %*% Q_slope` never sees `Sigma`.

So this is an **algebraic identity**, not an empirical finding about chr21/22.
No regime can break it — not MAF 0.01, not Z=30 — because `Sigma` is not in the
expression. Framing it as "tested only where max |z| is ~11" invites a search that
cannot find anything, and it does so while the real divergence (reported SE,
coverage, Type-I) goes unaddressed. The only residual risk is a *variant-set*
difference if a covariance path filters variants differently; on the OLS path
nothing does.

Two caveats that do bite: it holds only for `tau_cov_estimator = "ols"` (GLS uses
`Sigma` in the point estimate — `compute_gls_param_gwas`), and `Q_stat` and
`param_cov_upper` differ between the arms even under OLS. **What would make it
wrong:** any future change that puts `Sigma` on the OLS point-estimate path.
**Settled. Redirect the effort to item 10.**

### B. "`--loco-mode covariate` is better" — supported on the only trait where it barely does anything.

`LOCO_AS_COVARIATE.md` is admirably explicit about this: within-sex `R^2 = 0.0003`,
`r = 0.9999` against no-LOCO on `beta_left`/`beta_right`, "a near-no-op". So on
this trait the covariate form is validated as *harmless*, not as *better at
structure control*. The entire deconfounding case rests on `gamma` moving ~3% on
one chromosome of one trait (BACKLOG H12, `OPEN`).

**What would have to be true for it to be wrong.** On a trait whose step-1 ridge
learned genuine polygenic signal, `L` has real within-component content. The
covariate form then residualises the RIF columns *and the genotype* on `L`
(Frisch-Waugh). If `L` is even slightly correlated with the causal genotype at
the test locus — which LOCO is designed to prevent, but only chromosome-wise, not
LD-wise across chromosomes — the projection removes real signal from both sides.
The subtraction has the same exposure but with the coefficient pinned. Nobody has
tested a trait where `L` carries content, so nobody has seen the regime where the
two forms actually differ.

**An unchecked mechanism, new and now on by default.** `build_rif_residuals`
(`cli.py:704`) is `Q, _ = np.linalg.qr(X)` with **no rank or condition check**.
Householder QR returns an orthonormal `Q` even when `X` is rank-deficient, so
`check_orthonormal_basis` (`ORTHONORMAL_BASIS_TOL = 1e-8`) passes and `span(Q)`
is strictly *larger* than `span(X)` — an arbitrary extra direction is projected
out of every RIF column and out of the genotype. Today's change appends a column
(`cli.py:1475`) to that matrix, and `covariate_basis_rank` (`:2085`) is computed
as `X_full.shape[1] + 1`, a **count, not a rank**. For testosterone `L` is near-
orthogonal to age+PCs so this is almost certainly fine; for a trait whose ridge
loaded on covariate-correlated structure it is not, and nothing would say so.
**Cost of a guard: one `numpy.linalg.matrix_rank` / `cond` check, minutes.**

**Cheapest real test.** `log_bmi` — polygenic, `cor(y, loco)` genuinely
polygenic rather than a sex proxy, LOCO already on disk, and it is the trait
`LOCO_SCALE.md`/`ALLELE_CONVENTION.md` already validate against REGENIE. One
chr22 run in each mode. **Cost: a few node-hours.**

### C. "the estimator is calibrated" — see item 6. Scoped correctly it is a useful result; as stated it over-reaches in one direction and under-reports a 5% bias in the other.

### D. "G-MoQ is on the information bound" — see item 9. The 1-3% under-shoot is not a puzzle to be explained away; it is the measurement announcing its own resolution, which is 3%, which is the size of the effect.

## Judged settled; recorded so the effort is not spent again

- **pooled == jackknife point estimates under OLS.** Algebraic (Tier 4A). Not
  worth another run.
- **H1, lambda cannot adjudicate at N=400k.** The masking experiment is the right
  design and it is decisive. The problem is that `LOCO_MIXTURE_HAZARD.md` and
  `RELATEDNESS.md` have not been told (items 5, 7).
- **Density cancellation in the efficiency quadratic form** (`W' Sigma^-1 W = D'
  V^-1 D`, verified to 1.6e-14). An algebraic identity, independently explains
  the fourth-order-kernel null result. Sound.
- **The membership column is load-bearing (H5).** The `rho_max` diagnostic,
